# Using EML generator

1. Navigate to the email generator:

   <img width="1126" height="832" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8fd74a56-f255-402f-a712-e4ba84fc4992" />

2. Enter your artist name, release name, release date, release description, recommended for fans of into the EML generator:

   <img width="1128" height="481" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/136e7986-83cc-4175-90ba-2bcda18403c9" />

3. Review your email by selecting the preview first email button and checking the outputted text. In particular be careful of capitalisation, grammar and whitespace errors:

   <img width="1095" height="470" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6a0abcf1-8ed1-4160-a329-564a708c4a15" />

4. If required upload an album art to the email using the upload button:
   
<img width="1107" height="246" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2a7468b1-0dd7-4987-8268-1f736f029b5d" />

5. Now the fiddly bit... have your bandcamp codes and the promo list (AS A CSV) downloaded top your machine.
 
6. Open the bandcamp codes with a spreadsheet program such as Libreoffice Calc or Microsoft Excel.

7. Open the promo list csv with a text editor such as Notepad:

<img width="1362" height="701" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/399587e7-e191-487a-bc50-fa419f100cb1" />

8. Copy all of the csv into the recipients CSV box. Copy all of the codes into the codes box

9. Once you are ready, click the generate zip file button:

<img width="1092" height="434" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/97316935-852b-47a7-a5ac-7fcc6cbe2723" />

10. Your zip file will export filled with EML files and two other files: code assignment.csv & unused codes.txt. Copy the unused codes into the passed recordings codes spreadsheet on the drive.

NOTE: If at this point you do not have an email client such as outlook installed and you are not logged in as the label, download outlook and log in as passed.

11. Unzip your .zip file and view the eml files inside.

12. Make a new folder called "sent" - you do not have to do this, but it will help keep track of the files.

13. Select the top 5 eml files and cut/paste them into the sending folder.

14. Select the 5 new emails in the sending folder, right click them and select "open with outlook". Wait for all 5 emails to open.

 <img width="809" height="378" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3906773c-9cfb-4fb2-84e2-7f840997e14a" />

15. Now for each email: Review the email, personalise it if needed, and then click send.

16. Once all five are sent repeat the process with the next 5 - and so on - until all emails are sent.
